# Walkthrough video

The walkthrough is one real session on the deployed app (https://nativeproof.vercel.app), recorded on October 29, 2026 with the judging account. It uses live mode only: live Qloo data on the hackathon host and live models through the gateway. Nothing in it comes from demo fixtures. Synthetic participants are generated by the configured model and are labeled as AI-generated throughout the app.

The final video is in this folder, [`nativeproof-walkthrough.mp4`](nativeproof-walkthrough.mp4) (18 min 39 s), and is also attached to the [v1.0.0 release](https://github.com/vishavmehra/NativeProof/releases/tag/v1.0.0).

## Files

| File | What it is |

| [`assets/creative-trailhead-v1.png`](assets/creative-trailhead-v1.png) | The ad creative uploaded in the session, made from the Unsplash photo `frontend/public/imagery/outdoor-mountain-lake.webp` (Unsplash License; see `frontend/public/imagery/CREDITS.md`) |

## How it was made

1. The session was driven in a Chrome window and recorded per feature with the Chrome DevTools screencast (`demo/raw/`, not committed).
2. The voiceover was written after recording, from the outputs on screen, and synthesized with Microsoft Edge text to speech (`en-US-AndrewMultilingualNeural`) through `edge-tts`. It is a synthetic voice.
3. `build_video.py` speeds up long waits so each segment matches its voiceover, and stamps the real elapsed time and the playback speed in the top right corner. It adds a title card before each segment and burns in the captions.

To rebuild after re-recording: put the screen recordings in `demo/raw/` with the names listed in `segments.json`, install ffmpeg and uv, then run `python demo/build_video.py`.

## What the session shows, in order

Sign in; project, brand kit and campaign versions; Atlas discovery with five ambiguous Qloo terms confirmed by hand; a quick rehearsal; a 100-participant population study with an uploaded creative; its results, reaction graph and interviews; a three-round social simulation with stance changes and reasons, and its diffusion graph zoomed in; participant memory, a follow-up and a group question; Risk Radar with vision; Voice adaptations; Loop evaluation; the same 100 participants re-interviewed on a revised version; cancelling a run; a real restart of the production API in the middle of a study, followed by Resume from the last checkpoint; Campaign Arena; Cultural DNA; Market Expansion with ambiguous terms confirmed once for both markets; Brand Memory and Trend Watch; the Evidence Graph zoomed in; project search; and the Proof report.

Twelve architecture diagrams from [`diagrams/`](../diagrams) are narrated next to the features they explain: system architecture, deployment, authentication, the research workflow, the Qloo evidence pipeline, population generation, the social simulation, interviews and follow-ups, the model gateway, runs and recovery, the data model, and the Evidence Graph. Graph segments are cropped to the canvas so the links stay legible.
